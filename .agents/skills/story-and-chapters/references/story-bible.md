# Story bible — «Городской маршрут»

## Premise
The player becomes head of transport in a small town (Верхнереченск, ~5,000 people). Inheritance: two marshrutka vans, an old depot, and a folder from the predecessor who suddenly vanished. Notes inside: "Не соглашайся на метро до сорокового" and "Проверь, кто владеет землёй у вокзала".

## Characters
- Player: new head of transport; personality formed by choices.
- Степан Ильич: veteran driver, first ally; knows the city and what the predecessor knew, keeps silent at first.
- Лидия Воронцова: mayor; needs transport to succeed, has her own plans and election pressure.
- Артём Грач: developer; offers money for routes through his land. Main antagonist but really builds the city, not a cartoon villain.
- Вера Лунина: journalist; digs into the predecessor's story; can become ally or threat.
- Павел Корнеев: the predecessor; refused Грач's scheme and was pushed out.

## Chapters (unlock by population)
1. Посёлок (to 10k): save transport from bankruptcy; first routes; conflict with private minibus operators; meet Степан Ильич.
2. Рост (to 50k): Грач offers to fund buses if they run through his plots. Choice: take money / bargain / refuse.
3. Город (to 200k): documents show Корнеев refused Грач and was pushed out. Вера starts an investigation. Choice: reveal truth or not.
4. Агломерация (to 1M): metro construction. Power and huge money, pressure from politicians, unions and residents. Воронцова asks for support in elections.
5. Мегаполис (1M+): final choice — whose transport is it: citizens, the state, or private owners.

## Endings
- Город для людей: low fares kept, Грач's schemes exposed publicly. Transport loses money, the city loves you.
- Эффективная машина: profitable system, some districts sacrificed.
- Сделка: took Грач's money, became part of the system. Rich but uneasy.
- Уход: like Корнеев, you leave and pass the folder on. The circle closes.
Ending = function of flags (`took_grach_money`, `exposed_grach`, `rel.*`), final fares, trust, money.

## Tone
Grounded, human, slightly noir about city politics. No magic, no cartoon villains.
